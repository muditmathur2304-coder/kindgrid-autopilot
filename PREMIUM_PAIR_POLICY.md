COMMERCIAL METHOD
One PAID high-value workbook and one independently useful FREE companion per campaign. Paid is published and publicly verified FIRST. The free PDF's final page and Gumroad listing must link to the actual paid URL. Promote the free offer more often as an initial *hypothesis*, not proven optimal strategy. Experiment with specific unique Pinterest keywords and graphic hooks, not duplicate graphic spam.

CONTENT ACCEPTANCE
Paid normally 16–35 genuinely distinct instructional and worksheet pages; free 3–6 useful pages. Real worked examples with separately checked math, original informative diagrams, flow charts and filled examples, substantial editorial explanations and a clear buyer-specific decision framework, not repetitive blank worksheets. Premium 1000x1500 covers for each listing, at least two actual paid page preview images and one free inside-page preview; rendered full-document contact sheet for each PDF, independent review of every page and any spreadsheet formulas. Human-edited prose, no generic AI claims or fictitious testimonials/financial outcomes.

Before marking a new pair ready: a verifiable Google Docs Demand Evidence Report with BUYER PROBLEM, COMPETITOR, DIFFERENTIATION, EVIDENCE and LIMITATION headings, >=2 real source URLs and an honestly characterized Pinterest US search signal. Etsy result count alone cannot establish high buyer demand or low competition. No invented keyword volumes, conversion rates, seller earnings, or guarantees. Current testing priority is KGS-007 FREE -> KGS-006 PAID Paycheck Gap, already published. Other markets (e.g. micro-bakery farmers-market break-even) are only hypotheses pending Pinterest research.

Measure Pins DELIVERED vs SCHEDULED, impressions, outbound clicks, saves, and Gumroad UTM/referrer landing visits, free checkouts, paid sales at 48-hour / 7-day / 14-day / 30-day cohorts.
