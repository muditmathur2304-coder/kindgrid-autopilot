A custom art commission can be underpriced before the first sketch is made. The forgotten work is often around the artwork: negotiating scope, collecting references, estimating revisions, packing safely and explaining the final quote to a client.

COMMISSION READY is a practical quote-to-delivery system for independent painters, portrait makers, illustrators and other custom-art creators.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE
• 24-page illustrated US Letter PDF field guide, with a complete progression from first enquiry to client sign-off.
• Editable Excel workbook with Quote Builder, Job Review and Usage Notes tabs. Formulas calculate a suggested pre-tax quote, deposits, modeled selling fees and surplus based on your own inputs.
• Real, double-checked examples explaining the difference between markup and margin.
• Scope definition and exclusion checklist, time-budget method, revision boundaries, deposit stages, sample wording for change requests and customer-facing quote template.
• Helpful studio diagrams, before-and-after comparisons and reusable paperwork.
• An honest post-job review to compare estimated and actual hours/costs.
